How it works

Set it up once.
It runs every day.

Three steps. No loopholes.

Step 1

Pick the apps that waste your time

Instagram, TikTok, games — you choose. Froggy locks them at the time you set, every single day.

Step 2

Do your squat

Prop your phone up and squat. The camera tracks your hips and knees — the timer only counts while your hips are at or below knee level.

Step 3

Get your apps back

Hold it for 3 minutes and everything unlocks until tomorrow. Your streak grows and your depth score climbs.

Why a squat?

Most adults can't do this anymore

The deep squat is how humans rested for thousands of years. Sit in chairs long enough and you lose it — along with the ankle, knee, hip, and back mobility that goes with it.

4 joints,
one move

One deep squat works your ankles, knees, hips, and lower back — the four places that stiffen up first as you age.

3 min/day =
18 hrs/year

Three minutes sounds like nothing. Done every day, it adds up to 18 hours of mobility work a year — no gym, no equipment.

Improve
or decline

Range of motion doesn't hold steady. Every day you're either keeping it or losing it. Froggy makes sure you keep it.

Your ability to move is your age.
