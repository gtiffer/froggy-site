Why a squat?

The anti-aging position

The deep squat is how humans rested for most of history. Now most adults can't hold one at all — and losing it is how bodies get old.

4 joints,
one move

A deep squat builds strength and range of motion in your ankles, knees, hips, and lower back — the exact places that stiffen up first.

3 min/day =
18 hrs/year

Skipping one day feels harmless. But three minutes every morning compounds into 18 hours of mobility work a year. That's the whole trick.

Improve
or decline

Mobility doesn't stand still. Every day you're either building your range of motion or quietly losing it. Froggy makes sure it's the first one.

Your ability to move is your age.
